**Mals Ecommerce** sells through the Mals hosted cart. Add-to-cart buttons send the product to Mals, and visitors pay on Mals' own pages, so your site has no basket or checkout pages of its own.

## Set it up

1. Open **Mals Ecommerce Settings**.
2. Enter your **Cart user ID**, the **Add-to-cart URL** and the **Checkout URL**. The checkout address must contain `{CARTUSERID}`, which is replaced with your cart user ID.
3. Tick **Enabled**. The cart then shows in the header and the add-to-cart buttons work.
4. Optionally tick **Hide the item count when the cart is empty**.
5. Press **Save Settings**.
